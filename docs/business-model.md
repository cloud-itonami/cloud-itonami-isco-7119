# Business Model: Independent Framing Crew Scheduling and Logistics Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7119`
- ISCO-08: `7119`
- Occupation: Building Frame and Related Trades Workers Not Elsewhere Classified
- Social impact: worker-safety, local-jobs, housing-quality

## Customer

- independent framing crews
- small residential/commercial framing contractors

## Offer

- job-site work-order and materials-usage logging
- crew and task scheduling
- safety-concern surfacing
- framing-materials supply coordination

## Scope boundary

This actor coordinates JOB-SITE SCHEDULING/LOGISTICS ONLY. It never
performs framing work itself, never finalizes a
structural-framing-execution decision and never overrides a
site-safety officer's judgment — those remain human/on-site
decisions, gated by the Framing Crew Governor's closed op-allowlist
and scope-exclusion check.

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew scheduling fee

## Trust Controls

- no proposal may finalize a structural-framing-execution decision or
  override a site-safety officer's judgment (hard, permanent block)
- every surfaced safety concern (structural-integrity, fall-hazard or
  crew-fatigue) always escalates to human sign-off, regardless of
  advisor confidence
- no framing-materials order above the crew's registered cost ceiling
  without governor gate
- scheduling and logistics records are auditable, not editable
